spinr is a decision wheel. It is not a gambling app: there are no stakes, prizes or currency. It never goes online; only the in-app purchases go through the App Store.

Trial (guideline 3.1.1): on first launch the app shows the free trial offer. It states the duration (1 day), that the app stays locked afterwards, and the price of the one-time unlock. Two non-consumable in-app purchases:
- "spinr 1-Day Trial" (solutions.xapient.spinr.trial, price 0): tap "Start free trial". The full app opens.
- "Unlock spinr" (solutions.xapient.spinr.full_unlock): tap "Unlock for <price>" on the same screen (no need to wait for the trial to end). The app is unlocked permanently.
"Restore purchase" is on the same screen and calls AppStore.sync().

After the trial day the app shows a thank-you screen with the unlock purchase and "Restore purchase".

How to use: tap the wheel, press "Spin" or flick it. The list icon opens the saved wheels and templates; entries can be edited there. The gear icon opens the settings (haptics, sound). No account, no login, no network access.
