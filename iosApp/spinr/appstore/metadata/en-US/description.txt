What's for dinner, who pays, whose turn is it? spinr is a decision wheel that spins like a real one. Tap the wheel, press "Spin" or flick it: the wheel slows down and stops wherever the pointer lands. The final position is spread evenly around the whole circle, so every slice has the same chance.

Features
• 2 to 50 entries per wheel, several wheels saved
• Templates: food, yes/no, who pays?, weekdays, dice, number ranges, compass directions, coin flip, rock/paper/scissors, truth or dare, letters A–Z
• Every template becomes a normal wheel you can edit
• Pegs and a pointer flap with a tactile click, optional sound
• Large result with confetti
• "Remove": take drawn entries out for this round, for example to draw an order
• Pastel colours, neighbouring slices never match
• Portrait and landscape, iPhone and iPad, English and German

spinr is not a gambling app: no stakes, no prizes.
