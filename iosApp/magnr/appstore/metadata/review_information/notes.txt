magnr is a metal detector that reads the calibrated magnetometer through Core Motion device motion (no permission prompt, no location access). It never goes online; only the in-app purchases go through the App Store.

Trial (guideline 3.1.1): on first launch the app shows the free trial offer. It states the duration (1 day), that the app stays locked afterwards, and the price of the one-time unlock. Two non-consumable in-app purchases:
- "magnr 1-Day Trial" (solutions.xapient.magnr.trial, price 0): tap "Start free trial". The full app opens.
- "Unlock magnr" (solutions.xapient.magnr.full_unlock): tap "Unlock for <price>" on the same screen (no need to wait for the trial to end). The app is unlocked permanently.
"Restore purchase" is on the same screen and calls AppStore.sync().

After the trial day the app shows a thank-you screen with the unlock purchase and "Restore purchase".

How to use: move the top edge of the device slowly over a wall or near a screw, a nail or a magnet. The fan fills up and the deviation in microtesla rises. "Zero" takes the current field as the new baseline. magnr only detects iron, steel, nickel and magnets, as stated in the app. No account, no login, no network access.
