pulsr is a metronome. It never goes online. Only the in-app purchases go through the App Store.

Background audio (UIBackgroundModes audio): the metronome keeps clicking with the screen locked while it plays, so musicians can practise without the display on. It stops on a phone call or when headphones are unplugged. Nothing plays in the background unless the user started the metronome.

Trial (guideline 3.1.1): on first launch the app shows the free trial offer. It states the duration (1 day), that the app stays locked afterwards, and the price of the one-time unlock. Two non-consumable in-app purchases:
- "pulsr 1-Day Trial" (solutions.xapient.pulsr.trial, price 0): tap "Start free trial". The full app opens.
- "Unlock pulsr" (solutions.xapient.pulsr.full_unlock): tap "Unlock for <price>" on the same screen (no need to wait for the trial to end). The app is unlocked permanently.
"Restore purchase" is on the same screen and calls AppStore.sync().

After the trial day the app shows a thank-you screen with the unlock purchase and "Restore purchase".

How to use: tap "Start", turn the wheel or use -/+ and "Tap" to set the tempo. The gear icon opens the settings (sound, accent, keep screen on). No account, no login, no network access.
