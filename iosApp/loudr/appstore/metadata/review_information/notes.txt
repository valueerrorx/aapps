loudr is a sound level meter. It uses the microphone only to compute the current level on the device, only while the app is in the foreground. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent; the app never goes online, only the in-app purchases go through the App Store.

Microphone permission: requested once on first launch with an explanation (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription). If declined, the app shows a note with a button that opens the Settings app.

Trial (guideline 3.1.1): on first launch the app shows the free trial offer. It states the duration (1 day), that the app stays locked afterwards, and the price of the one-time unlock. Two non-consumable in-app purchases:
- "loudr 1-Day Trial" (solutions.xapient.loudr.trial, price 0): tap "Start free trial". The full app opens.
- "Unlock loudr" (solutions.xapient.loudr.full_unlock): tap "Unlock for <price>" on the same screen (no need to wait for the trial to end). The app is unlocked permanently.
"Restore purchase" is on the same screen and calls AppStore.sync().

After the trial day the app shows a thank-you screen with the unlock purchase and "Restore purchase".

How to use: allow the microphone, the level appears right away. Pause/resume and reset are below the gauge. The gear icon opens the settings (time weighting, calibration offset). No account, no login, no network access.
