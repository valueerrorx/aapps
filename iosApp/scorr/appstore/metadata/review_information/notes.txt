scorr is a score keeper for board games, card games and table tennis. It never goes online; only the in-app purchases go through the App Store.

Trial (guideline 3.1.1): on first launch the app shows the free trial offer. It states the duration (1 day), that the app stays locked afterwards, and the price of the one-time unlock. Two non-consumable in-app purchases:
- "scorr 1-Day Trial" (solutions.xapient.scorr.trial, price 0): tap "Start free trial". The full app opens.
- "Unlock scorr" (solutions.xapient.scorr.full_unlock): tap "Unlock for <price>" on the same screen (no need to wait for the trial to end). The app is unlocked permanently.
"Restore purchase" is on the same screen and calls AppStore.sync().

After the trial day the app shows a thank-you screen with the unlock purchase and "Restore purchase".

How to use: each player has a colour tile. Tap the top half for +1, the bottom half for -1, drag up or down to count in steps, long-press to type a number. The round label at the top opens the history table, the arrow undoes the last move, the gear icon opens the settings (players, target, rounds, scoring). No account, no login, no network access.
