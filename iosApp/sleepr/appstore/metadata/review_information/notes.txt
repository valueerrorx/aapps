sleepr is a noise generator. All sound is computed live on the device (no audio files). It never goes online; only the in-app purchases go through the App Store.

Background audio: playback continues with the screen locked (UIBackgroundModes audio). It stops on interruptions (e.g. calls), when headphones are unplugged, from the lock screen controls, or when the optional timer ends with a gentle fade-out.

Trial (guideline 3.1.1): on first launch the app shows the free trial offer. It states the duration (1 day), that the app stays locked afterwards, and the price of the one-time unlock. Two non-consumable in-app purchases:
- "sleepr 1-Day Trial" (solutions.xapient.sleepr.trial, price 0): tap "Start free trial". The full app opens.
- "Unlock sleepr" (solutions.xapient.sleepr.full_unlock): tap "Unlock for <price>" on the same screen (no need to wait for the trial to end). The app is unlocked permanently.
"Restore purchase" is on the same screen and calls AppStore.sync().

After the trial day the app shows a thank-you screen with the unlock purchase and "Restore purchase".

How to use: press "Start". Mix the noise colours with the sliders, tap a mix (Ocean, Focus, ...) or the wave button next to a colour, pick a timer. The gear icon opens the settings with explanations. No account, no login, no network access.
