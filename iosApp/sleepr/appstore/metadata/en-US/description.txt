sleepr generates noise live on your device and lets you mix five noise colours freely: to mask distracting sounds, to focus or to fall asleep. There are no audio files and no loops. The noise is computed continuously and never repeats.

Features
• Five noise colours, each with its own slider: white, pink, brown, grey (shaped to human hearing) and green
• Wave mode per colour: the noise rises and falls like ocean waves, wave length 3 to 20 seconds
• Six ready-made mixes: Ocean, Focus, Sleep, Mask, Nature, Classic white
• Volume and a treble control for a darker or brighter sound
• Spectrum display of the current mix
• Timer from 15 to 90 minutes with a gentle fade-out at the end
• Keeps playing with the screen locked, stop it from the lock screen
• Portrait and landscape, iPhone and iPad, English and German

sleepr is not a medical app. Choose a comfortable, moderate volume, especially with headphones.

sleepr asks for no permissions.
