thinkr is a collection of nine short brain games (about a minute per round). It never goes online. Only the in-app purchases go through the App Store.

Trial (guideline 3.1.1): on first launch the app shows the free trial offer. It states the duration (1 day), that the app stays locked afterwards, and the price of the one-time unlock. Two non-consumable in-app purchases:
- "thinkr 1-Day Trial" (solutions.xapient.thinkr.trial, price 0): tap "Start free trial". The full app opens.
- "Unlock thinkr" (solutions.xapient.thinkr.full_unlock): tap "Unlock for <price>" on the same screen (no need to wait for the trial to end). The app is unlocked permanently.
"Restore purchase" is on the same screen and calls AppStore.sync().

After the trial day the app shows a thank-you screen with the unlock purchase and "Restore purchase".

How to use: the start screen shows today's three games, tap the first one to play. The games icon at the top lists all nine games, the gear icon opens the settings (relaxed mode, colour-blind mode, haptics, sounds). No account, no login, no network access. thinkr is entertainment and makes no health claims.
