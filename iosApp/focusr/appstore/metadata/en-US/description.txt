With focusr you see the time left at a glance: a coloured disc shows how much time remains and slowly shrinks as it runs down. You set the time by dragging the edge of the disc. Made for studying, meetings, cooking or the classroom, including a projector in landscape.

Features
• Set the time by dragging, with tactile detents
• Scale of 5, 20, 60 or 120 minutes
• Large time remaining and end time ("Ends at 2:35 PM")
• Start, pause, resume and reset
• A soft chime and vibration at the end, each can be turned off
• The end arrives on time, even with the screen locked or the app closed (as a notification with the chime)
• Screen stays on while the timer runs (can be turned off)
• Portrait and landscape, iPhone and iPad, English and German, works with VoiceOver

Permissions: only notifications, so that "Time's up" also reaches you when focusr is in the background. While the app is open, the timer works without them.
