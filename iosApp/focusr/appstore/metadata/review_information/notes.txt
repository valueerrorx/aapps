focusr is a visual timer: the remaining time is shown as a coloured disc that slowly shrinks. It never goes online; only the in-app purchases go through the App Store.

Trial (guideline 3.1.1): on first launch the app shows the free trial offer. It states the duration (1 day), that the app stays locked afterwards, and the price of the one-time unlock. Two non-consumable in-app purchases:
- "focusr 1-Day Trial" (solutions.xapient.focusr.trial, price 0): tap "Start free trial". The full app opens.
- "Unlock focusr" (solutions.xapient.focusr.full_unlock): tap "Unlock for <price>" on the same screen (no need to wait for the trial to end). The app is unlocked permanently.
"Restore purchase" is on the same screen and calls AppStore.sync().

After the trial day the app shows a thank-you screen with the unlock purchase and "Restore purchase".

How to use: drag around the disc to set the time, then press "Start". The app asks for notification permission on the first start; it is only used for the local "Time's up" notification with a chime when the app is in the background. The gear icon opens the settings (dial scale, chime, vibration). No account, no login, no network access.
