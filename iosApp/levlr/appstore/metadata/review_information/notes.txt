levlr is a spirit level that uses the motion sensor (no permission prompt needed). It never goes online; only the in-app purchases go through the App Store.

Trial (guideline 3.1.1): on first launch the app shows the free trial offer. It states the duration (1 day), that the app stays locked afterwards, and the price of the one-time unlock. Two non-consumable in-app purchases:
- "levlr 1-Day Trial" (solutions.xapient.levlr.trial, price 0): tap "Start free trial". The full app opens.
- "Unlock levlr" (solutions.xapient.levlr.full_unlock): tap "Unlock for <price>" on the same screen (no need to wait for the trial to end). The app is unlocked permanently.
"Restore purchase" is on the same screen and calls AppStore.sync().

After the trial day the app shows a thank-you screen with the unlock purchase and "Restore purchase".

How to use: lay the device flat or stand it on an edge. The big number shows the tilt; at 0.0° it locks in. Settings (gear icon): unit, haptics, two-point calibration, info. No account, no login, no network access.
