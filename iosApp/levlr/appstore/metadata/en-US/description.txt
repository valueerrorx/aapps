levlr shows you how far off level something is. Stand your iPhone or iPad on an edge or lay it flat and levlr picks the right mode by itself: a tube level with one axis on the edge, a bullseye level with two axes when lying flat.

At 0.0° the display locks in: the colour changes, a short shine runs across and you feel a tap. You can tell it is level without looking.

Features
• Switches automatically between edge and flat
• Large reading with one decimal place, in degrees, percent or mm/m
• Bubble and liquid move with spring physics
• Hold: freeze the reading to read it calmly
• Two-point calibration per mode, can be reset at any time
• Screen stays on while the level is open
• Portrait and landscape, iPad with Split View, English and German

Handy for hanging pictures, mounting shelves or levelling tables. Accuracy depends on your device's sensor. levlr is not meant for surveying work.
