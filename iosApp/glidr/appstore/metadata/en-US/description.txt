Cool Cows on Ice is a physics puzzle for relaxed brain-teasing. The cows slide across the ice with real physics, and ramps, water holes and spike blocks stand in their way. Work out how to beat each level and collect stars.

• 48 levels with real physics
• Stars for every level, your progress is saved
• With music and sound effects, in landscape

Free: no ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking. The game never goes online and runs completely offline. Your progress stays on your device.
