glidr (Cool Cows on Ice) is a free physics puzzle game: an HTML5 canvas game (Phaser) bundled with the app and shown in a WKWebView. All game files ship inside the app bundle and are served through a custom URL scheme; the app never goes online and has no in-app purchases, ads or accounts.

How to play: pick a level in the menu, then touch and drag on the ice to get the sliding cows back into their paddocks. Progress and stars are stored on the device (WebView local storage). Landscape only.
