hertzr is a tone generator. All sound is computed live on the device (no audio files). It never goes online; only the in-app purchases go through the App Store.

Background audio: tones and sweeps keep playing with the screen locked (UIBackgroundModes audio), like the foreground service of the Android version. Playback stops on interruptions (e.g. calls), when headphones are unplugged, from the lock screen controls or when a sweep has finished.

Trial (guideline 3.1.1): on first launch the app shows the free trial offer. It states the duration (1 day), that the app stays locked afterwards, and the price of the one-time unlock. Two non-consumable in-app purchases:
- "hertzr 1-Day Trial" (solutions.xapient.hertzr.trial, price 0): tap "Start free trial". The full app opens.
- "Unlock hertzr" (solutions.xapient.hertzr.full_unlock): tap "Unlock for <price>" on the same screen (no need to wait for the trial to end). The app is unlocked permanently.
"Restore purchase" is on the same screen and calls AppStore.sync().

After the trial day the app shows a thank-you screen with the unlock purchase and "Restore purchase".

How to use: press the play button. Drag the frequency band or use −/+ and the octave buttons, pick a waveform, set the level. "Sweep" plays a logarithmic sweep. The gear icon opens the settings (output channels, keep screen on). Please start at a low volume. No account, no login, no network access.
