tunr is a chromatic tuner. It uses the microphone only to detect the pitch on the device, only while the tuning screen is in the foreground. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent; the app never goes online, only the in-app purchases go through the App Store.

Microphone permission: requested once on first launch with an explanation (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription). If declined, the app shows a note with a button that opens the Settings app.

Trial (guideline 3.1.1): on first launch the app shows the free trial offer. It states the duration (1 day), that the app stays locked afterwards, and the price of the one-time unlock. Two non-consumable in-app purchases:
- "tunr 1-Day Trial" (solutions.xapient.tunr.trial, price 0): tap "Start free trial". The full app opens.
- "Unlock tunr" (solutions.xapient.tunr.full_unlock): tap "Unlock for <price>" on the same screen (no need to wait for the trial to end). The app is unlocked permanently.
"Restore purchase" is on the same screen and calls AppStore.sync().

After the trial day the app shows a thank-you screen with the unlock purchase and "Restore purchase".

How to use: allow the microphone and play or sing a note; the note and its deviation appear right away. Tap the instrument name to pick guitar, bass, ukulele or violin; tap a string to hear its reference tone. The gear icon opens the settings (concert pitch, note names, haptics). No account, no login, no network access.
