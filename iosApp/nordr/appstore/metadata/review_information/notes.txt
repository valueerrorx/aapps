nordr is a compass that uses Core Motion device motion with the magnetic-north reference frame (no permission prompt, no location access). It never goes online; only the in-app purchases go through the App Store.

Trial (guideline 3.1.1): on first launch the app shows the free trial offer. It states the duration (1 day), that the app stays locked afterwards, and the price of the one-time unlock. Two non-consumable in-app purchases:
- "nordr 1-Day Trial" (solutions.xapient.nordr.trial, price 0): tap "Start free trial". The full app opens.
- "Unlock nordr" (solutions.xapient.nordr.full_unlock): tap "Unlock for <price>" on the same screen (no need to wait for the trial to end). The app is unlocked permanently.
"Restore purchase" is on the same screen and calls AppStore.sync().

After the trial day the app shows a thank-you screen with the unlock purchase and "Restore purchase".

How to use: hold the device flat or upright. The big number shows the heading. "Set course" marks the current direction and shows the deviation. True north uses a region picked in the settings (gear icon) and the built-in World Magnetic Model, no location needed. No account, no login, no network access.
