Was auf deinem Gerät gespeichert wird

Die Apps speichern Einstellungen und Spielstände lokal im privaten App-Speicher, zum Beispiel Lautstärke und Mischung (sleepr), Kalibrierung (levlr, loudr), Räder (spinr), Spielernamen und Punkte (scorr), gespeicherte Trainings (roundr), Timer-Zustand (focusr, dozr), Rekord und Sterne (moovr, glidr). Diese Daten verlassen das Gerät nicht und sind für den Anbieter nicht zugänglich. Beim Deinstallieren der App werden sie gelöscht; du kannst sie auch jederzeit in den Android-Einstellungen über „Speicher leeren“ löschen.

Fast alle Apps sind von der Android-Systemsicherung ausgenommen. Nur sleepr erlaubt sie: Ist die Sicherung auf deinem Gerät eingeschaltet, kann Android die Einstellungen von sleepr in deine eigene Gerätesicherung übernehmen. Das macht das System, nicht die App, und der Anbieter hat darauf keinen Zugriff.

What is stored on your device

The apps store settings and game progress locally in their private app storage, for example volume and mix (sleepr), calibration (levlr, loudr), wheels (spinr), player names and scores (scorr), saved workouts (roundr), timer state (focusr, dozr), best distance and stars (moovr, glidr). This data never leaves the device and is not accessible to the provider. It is deleted when you uninstall the app, and you can delete it at any time with "Clear storage" in the Android settings.

Almost all apps are excluded from Android system backup. Only sleepr allows it: if backup is turned on on your device, Android may include sleepr's settings in your own device backup. This is done by the system, not by the app, and the provider has no access to it.

Google Play

When you install an app through Google Play, Google processes data under its own terms. The provider only receives the usual anonymous or aggregated Play Console statistics and, where applicable, purchase records from Google. The apps themselves send nothing.

Trial and purchase

The tool apps (all except moovr and glidr) can be used fully for 24 hours from the first start and can then be unlocked for good with a one-time in-app purchase. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play: the app asks the Play Store app on your device through Google Play billing (permission "Google Play billing service") whether the app has been bought. The app itself never goes online and only learns "bought" or "not bought". Only the time of the first start and whether the app is unlocked are stored locally. Payment data is processed by Google alone; the provider does not receive it.
