pulsr is a metronome for practice. The clicks are mixed into the audio stream with sample accuracy instead of being triggered by a timer. That keeps the beat steady, and it does not drift even over long sessions.

Features
• Tempo from 20 to 300 BPM: turn the wheel, use −/+ or tap tempo
• Time signatures with 1 to 12 beats, accented first beat (can be turned off)
• Subdivisions: quarters, eighths, triplets or sixteenths
• Three sounds: click, wood and beep
• Change tempo or time signature while playing without stumbling
• Light pulse in sync with the sound, with tempo markings from Largo to Prestissimo
• Keeps playing with the screen locked and stops when you unplug your headphones or a call comes in
• Mixes with music that is already playing, so you can practise along to recordings
• Screen stays on while playing (can be turned off)
• Portrait and landscape, English and German

Permissions: notifications (stop button) and a foreground service so the metronome keeps playing with the screen locked. pulsr also works without notifications. Plus Google Play billing for the one-time purchase.
