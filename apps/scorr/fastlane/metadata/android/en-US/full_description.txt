scorr keeps score for board and card games, table tennis or quiz nights. Every player or team gets a big colour tile. Tap the top half for plus one, the bottom half for minus one. For bigger jumps, drag up or down across the tile, or long-press and type in a number.

Features
• 1 to 8 players or teams, each with a name and a colour
• Quick changes are combined into one move, unlimited undo
• Rounds (optional) with a history table and round winners
• Scoring by rounds won or total points
• Optional target, winner with the most or the fewest points
• The game is kept even when the app is closed
• Screen stays on (can be turned off)
• Portrait and landscape, English and German

Player names and scores are stored only on your device.

scorr needs no permissions other than vibration and Google Play billing.
