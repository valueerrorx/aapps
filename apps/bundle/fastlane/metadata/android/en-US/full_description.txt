xapient bundle is the key to all xapient apps. Install it from Google Play and every xapient app on your device is unlocked, including future ones, without buying each one separately.

How it works
• Buy and install xapient bundle once.
• Install the xapient apps you like or keep the ones you already have. At their next start they find the bundle and open fully unlocked, without a trial screen.
• Keep xapient bundle installed. On a new phone, install it again from Google Play with the same Google account, no second purchase needed.

One-time purchase, no subscription, no ads.

• Private: no tracking, no account, no data collected. xapient bundle does not make money from your data.
