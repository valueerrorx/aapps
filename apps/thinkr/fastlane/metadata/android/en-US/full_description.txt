thinkr brings nine short brain games for a quick break, each round takes about a minute. Every day thinkr picks three of them as your daily session, and you can play all the others freely from the path.

The games
• Echo: Is the shape the same as the previous one? Later you compare with the one two back.
• Switches: Steer beads of light over switches into the gate with their colour and shape.
• Constellation: Remember where the stars lit up.
• Ink: Does the meaning of one word match the ink colour of the other?
• Swarm: Swipe where the middle comet points.
• Bounce: Where does the light leave after the prisms bend it?
• Drops: Solve the sums before they fall into the water.
• Glance: A shape in the centre and a star at the edge, caught in a blink.
• Slalom: Steer your glider past pillars and rocks in the crystal canyon.

Features
• Levels adapt to how you play, best scores per game
• Daily session with a streak, a missed day is now and then bridged quietly
• Relaxed mode with more time and its own levels
• Colour-blind mode for Ink, subtle sounds optional
• Portrait and landscape, English and German
