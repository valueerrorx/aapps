Clumsy Cow is a skill game for a quick break. A clumsy cow hops across drifting ice floes, and you help her get as far as she can.

• Jump and push, dodge the spikes
• Collect clover leaves for extra lives and stars for cow superpowers
• Your best distance in metres is saved
• With music and sound effects, in landscape

No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking. The game has no internet permission and runs completely offline. Your record stays on your device.
