levlr shows you how far off level something is. Stand your phone on an edge or lay it flat and levlr picks the right mode by itself: a tube level with one axis on the edge, a bullseye level with two axes when lying flat.

At 0.0° the display locks in: the colour changes, a short shine runs across and the phone vibrates. You can tell it is level without looking.

Features
• Switches automatically between edge and flat
• Large reading with one decimal place, in degrees, percent or mm/m
• Bubble and liquid move with spring physics
• Hold: freeze the reading to read it calmly
• Two-point calibration per mode, can be reset at any time
• Screen stays on while the level is open
• Portrait and landscape, English and German

Handy for hanging pictures, mounting shelves or levelling tables. Accuracy depends on your phone's sensor. levlr is not meant for surveying work.

levlr needs no permissions other than vibration.

No ads, no tracking, no account. levlr has no internet permission, everything stays on your device.
