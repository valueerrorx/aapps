dividr splits expenses in a group, on a sailing trip, on holiday or in a shared flat. No account, no server, no internet: phones swap everything by QR code. One person creates the group, the others scan the code and join.

Features
• Expenses for everyone or for selected people, split evenly
• Payments to settle up, other currencies with an exchange rate
• People who join later share from the start or only from now on
• Anyone can edit any entry, the import shows every change and deletion
• Settle up with as few transfers as possible, the same plan on every phone
• Share the settlement as text via any messenger
• Guests without a phone, merge duplicate people
• Several groups, portrait and landscape, English and German

A code contains everything your phone knows about the group. Scan each other and you are on the same page.

dividr only uses the camera to scan the codes.

No ads, no tracking, no account. dividr has no internet permission, everything stays on your devices.
