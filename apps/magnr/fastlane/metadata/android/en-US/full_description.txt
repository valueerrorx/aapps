magnr uses your phone's magnetic field sensor to find screws, nails, steel beams and magnets nearby. A fan of eight arcs shows how far the field deviates from the baseline, with the deviation in microtesla below.

What magnr finds and what it does not: it only detects ferromagnetic metals (iron, steel, nickel) and magnets. It does not detect gold, silver, copper or aluminium. magnr is not a wire or pipe detector: cables and pipes may go unnoticed. The sensor usually sits near the top of the phone, so move the top edge slowly across the surface.

Features
• Deviation from the baseline in µT, plus field strength, baseline and peak
• Three sensitivity levels
• Zero at the push of a button and slow drift compensation at rest (can be turned off)
• Compensates a residual sensor offset when tilting, if the phone has an orientation sensor
• Vibration as the level rises, optional beeps
• History of the last 20 seconds
• Calibration hint and a notice when the sensor is saturated
• Portrait and landscape, English and German

magnr needs a magnetic field sensor and no permissions other than vibration.

No ads, no tracking, no account. magnr has no internet permission, everything stays on your device.
