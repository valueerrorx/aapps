roundr switches between work and rest for you and counts the rounds. The whole screen glows in the colour of the current phase and drains like a fill level, with the time left in large digits in the middle. You can see how long is left even from a few metres away.

Features
• Prepare, work, rest, rounds, sets and set rest, all adjustable
• Templates: Tabata, Tabata with 3 sets, HIIT 40/20, EMOM, boxing 3:00/1:00
• Save up to 20 of your own workouts by name
• 3-2-1 beeps before every phase change, separate sounds for work, rest and finish, plus vibration (both can be turned off)
• Keeps running with the screen locked, controls in the notification
• Pause, skip to the next phase or go back to the start of the phase
• Time runs steadily without drifting
• Screen stays on during a workout (can be turned off)
• Portrait and landscape, English and German

roundr is a timer. It does not record health or fitness data and gives no training advice.

Permissions: notifications, vibration and a foreground service so the timer keeps running with the screen locked. The timer also works without notifications.

No ads, no tracking, no account. roundr has no internet permission, everything stays on your device.
