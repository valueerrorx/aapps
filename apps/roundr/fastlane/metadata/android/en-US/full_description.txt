roundr switches between active and rest phases for you and counts the rounds. The whole screen glows in the colour of the current phase and drains like a fill level, with the time left in large digits in the middle. You can see how long is left even from a few metres away.

Circuit training, a board game with a time limit, study sessions with breaks or the steps of a recipe: you set the phases and rounds, roundr tells you when to move on.

Features
• Prepare, active, rest, rounds, blocks and block break, all adjustable
• Templates: Tabata, Tabata with 3 blocks, HIIT 40/20, EMOM, boxing 3:00/1:00, game round 2:00/0:30, study 25/5
• Save up to 20 of your own timers by name
• 3-2-1 beeps before every phase change, separate sounds for active, rest and finish, plus vibration (both can be turned off)
• Keeps running with the screen locked, controls in the notification
• Pause, skip to the next phase or go back to the start of the phase
• Time runs steadily without drifting
• Screen stays on while a timer is running (can be turned off)
• Portrait and landscape, English and German

roundr is a timer. It does not record health or fitness data and gives no advice.
