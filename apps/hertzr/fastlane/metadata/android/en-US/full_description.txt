hertzr generates a clean tone with a precisely adjustable frequency and waveform. For speaker and subwoofer tests, finding room resonances, exploring your own hearing range or physics lessons.

Features
• Frequency from 1 Hz to 22 kHz (the upper limit depends on the device's sample rate)
• Adjust with a logarithmic frequency band, −/+, octave steps or direct input
• Shows the nearest note name with its deviation in cents
• Sine, square, triangle and sawtooth waveforms with anti-aliasing
• Sweep: logarithmic from start to end frequency in 5 to 60 seconds
• Output on both channels, left only, right only or inverted (polarity test)
• Oscilloscope display of the waveform
• Keeps playing with the screen off and stops when you unplug your headphones or a call comes in
• Portrait and landscape, English and German

Protect your ears and speakers: hertzr starts at −24 dB, fades in and out smoothly, warns at high levels and turns the level down when headphones are connected during playback. Always start quietly anyway.

hertzr is a technical tool, with no "healing frequencies" and no health claims.
